About the Creator

Made by a Teacher,
for Teachers

Hi! I'm the educator behind Little Pillo Academy. I earned my Bachelor's in Education (English specialty) from Universidad de Los Andes in Venezuela, and began my teaching journey at Instituto Andes in Caracas in 1998.

After moving to the United States in 2001, I earned my Texas Bilingual Education certification and spent nearly a decade teaching PreK in Tomball and Spring ISD — nurturing hundreds of young learners in both English and Spanish.

After stepping back to raise my family, I wanted to give back. Every worksheet is designed from scratch by me — no AI, no shortcuts — built on real classroom experience and a deep love for early literacy.
